## The core strategic decisions (do not re-debate these)

**Brand idea:** "Care for the line that runs you." The line is the spine. The spine carries the signal. The signal runs everything.

**Recommended concept:** C — Living System. Anatomical illustration as brand signature asset. Bone/Olive palette. GT Sectra or Tiempos Headline serif + Söhne/Inter grotesque.

**Retired from the brand:** Vivid emerald green (current logo colour). Pure white. Pure black. Wellness clichés in copy.

**Palette (Concept C):**
- Bone #EDE7DA — primary background
- Soft Olive Cream #F2F4EE — secondary background
- Deep Olive Moss #5F6F4A — brand colour
- Olive Bark #2F3A23 — type/anchor
- Clay #B89576 — warm accent, illustration tone only
- Slate Black #1F211C — type on photography only

**Tagline (recommended):** "Care for the line that runs you."
**Secondary warm line:** "Known by name. Looked after by hand."

## Three audience archetypes

1. **Recovering high-functioning professional** (40%) — late 20s to mid-40s, desk-based, chronic pain, expects to understand the plan, pays for quality
2. **Active body wanting to stay active** (30%) — 30s to 60s, sports-oriented, pragmatic, wants protocol clarity
3. **Optimiser without a problem** (30%, growing) — wellness-curious, nervous system regulation, treats chiropractic as long-term investment
